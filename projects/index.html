Alteturia Games

MULTIPLAYER GAMING PLATFORM

Alteturia Games brings familiar games into a shared digital experience, helping friends connect through play in the same room or remotely.

The platform combines Nigerian favourites such as Whot and Ayo with word, board and social games, including Imposter, Police Catch Thief, and Name Animal Place Thing. Players can host or join multiplayer rooms using room codes, invitation links or QR codes.

Designed around mobile use, the platform supports guest access alongside player accounts and profiles. In-game chat and reactions add a social layer, making conversation and interaction part of the experience.

The project reflects Alteturia Studios’ interest in creating accessible digital products that connect local culture with everyday entertainment.

DEVELOPMENT PROJECT

The Edible Shop

ONLINE FOOD MARKETPLACEIN DEVELOPMENT

The Edible Shop brings independent food businesses and shoppers together in one online marketplace. Discover food products, explore stores and order what you need, with a place to manage purchases and get help along the way.

For shoppers: Browse products and seller profiles, build your basket and place orders. Keep delivery addresses, order history and support conversations together in your account, and share feedback through product reviews after delivery.

For food businesses: Reach customers through your own store. Apply to become a seller and, once approved, publish products with photographs, prices and stock information. Manage your catalogue and order fulfilment as your business grows.

For marketplace operators: Keep store applications, accounts, listings, orders and payment records organized. Support tickets bring enquiries, replies and resolutions into one place, making it easier to follow up on customer and seller needs.

Graqivo

IN DEVELOPMENT

Graqivo brings projects, documents and ongoing work into one shared workspace, helping your team see what belongs together and find the information behind each project.

Keep documents connected to the work. Graqivo is being built around project-centred organization, so teams can understand how files support their projects while keeping an organization-wide view of their documents.

Give your organization its own space. The planned platform gives each organization a separate workspace with its own members and administrative roles. The person who creates the organization becomes its first administrator and can appoint others to help manage it.

A clearer view for your team. Designed for organizations that want less scattered information and more context around their work, Graqivo aims to make project visibility, information organization and collaboration easier.
