Alteturia Games

MULTIPLAYER GAMING PLATFORM

Alteturia Games brings familiar games into a shared digital experience, helping friends connect through play in the same room or remotely.

The platform combines Nigerian favourites such as Whot and Ayo with word, board and social games, including Imposter, Police Catch Thief, and Name Animal Place Thing. Players can host or join multiplayer rooms using room codes, invitation links or QR codes.

Designed around mobile use, the platform supports guest access alongside player accounts and profiles. In-game chat and reactions add a social layer, making conversation and interaction part of the experience.

The project reflects Alteturia Studios’ interest in creating accessible digital products that connect local culture with everyday entertainment.

DEVELOPMENT PROJECT

The Edible Shop

ONLINE FOOD MARKETPLACE

Developed by Alteturia Studios for Crop Edibles.

The Edible Shop is a web-based food marketplace designed to help independent businesses bring their products to more customers. It connects product discovery and shopping with the tools sellers need to manage their stores.

Customers can browse food products, explore seller profiles, manage a shopping basket and place orders. Customer accounts bring together delivery addresses, order history and support conversations, while product reviews help shoppers share feedback after delivery.

Businesses can apply to become sellers, submit their store information for review and publish products once approved. Seller tools support product photographs, pricing, stock management and order fulfilment.

For marketplace administrators, the platform provides oversight of store applications, accounts, listings, orders and payment records. A support ticket system keeps enquiries and order-related conversations organised, with a record of replies and resolutions.

The project demonstrates Alteturia Studios’ ability to build connected digital commerce experiences, combining a public storefront with the operational workflows behind a marketplace.

DEVELOPMENT PROJECT

Graqivo

IN DEVELOPMENT

Graqivo is a workspace platform being developed to help organizations organize their projects and the documents that support them.

The project addresses a practical challenge: teams often have files spread across different locations, making it difficult to understand how those files connect to ongoing work. Graqivo places projects at the centre of that experience while retaining an organization-wide file overview.

Its planned structure supports multiple organizations, each with its own workspace, members and administrative roles. The person who registers an organization becomes its initial administrator and can assign additional administrators to help manage the workspace.

Graqivo is being shaped around clearer project visibility, organized information and collaboration within each organization. Initial testing is intended for Alteturia and Smart Agribus, providing practical feedback to guide further development.
